Flick Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 13, 2026

Flick is a book ranking and reading library app.

Data Stored On Your Device

Flick stores your reading library, reading status, rankings, profile details, notes, reviews, and profile photo locally on your device.

Data Sent Off Device

When you search for books, Flick may send book search terms, titles, authors, ISBNs, or public book identifiers to its book metadata backend to fetch public book information and covers.

Accounts and Tracking

Flick does not currently use accounts, advertising, tracking, or cloud sync for your reading profile.
