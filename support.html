Flick Support

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Get help with your reading library, ranking, book search and imports.

Contact Flick’s developer

nazlizonoozi@gmail.com

Describe what happened and what you expected. Your iPhone model, iOS version and Flick version/build (shown in TestFlight for beta builds) can help. Screenshots are optional; hide any private information first.

A book is missing or has the wrong details

Try searching for the title and author together, or scan/type the ISBN from the book. Editions and covers can differ. If the book is still wrong or missing, use Flick’s book-report action where available, or email the title, author, ISBN and a short description of the problem.

Search or covers are not loading

Check your internet connection and try again. Book information and covers depend on online catalogue services, which can be slow or unavailable. Keep your existing library installed while reporting an issue.

Goodreads sync or import needs attention

In Profile → Settings → Goodreads sync, check that the connected public profile is yours and try Sync. Goodreads feeds can be delayed or incomplete. For a full-library import, use a Goodreads CSV export with Flick’s Goodreads import option.

Some books need matching or ranking after import. Review the Goodreads entries in your Library. Disconnecting Goodreads stops future checks and leaves already imported books in Flick; it does not change your Goodreads shelves. Never send us your Goodreads password.

Importing a book list from Notes

Use Import from Notes and paste a book list with one book per line. Including the author helps Flick find the correct edition. Flick reads the text you paste, not your Notes library. The text is used for online book matching, so avoid pasting private notes.

Keeping or restoring your library

Use Profile → Settings → Your data → Export my Flick data to save a private backup to Files. To restore a saved Flick backup, use Import my Flick data and review the replacement confirmation carefully. Exports can contain private notes, profile details and Goodreads connection information; new exports do not include your profile photo.

Flick does not currently sync your library to a Flick account. Do not delete or reinstall the app as a troubleshooting step before making a backup. An installation problem or a lost device cannot be repaired by downloading your library from a Flick server.

A book reminder is unavailable

Generated reminders need a supported device and an available Apple on-device model. Flick can show a catalogue description when generation is unavailable or still preparing. An internet connection may be needed to retrieve that description. A generated reminder may be inaccurate; report a problem with the book title and what looked wrong.

Camera permission

The scanner reads ISBN barcodes, rather than photographing a book cover. You can manage camera access for Flick in iOS Settings or use typed search instead.

Privacy questions or requests

Read the Privacy Policy for details about local data, book lookups and optional services. Email nazlizonoozi@gmail.com for a privacy question or request. Please do not attach your whole reading backup unless you intend to share its contents.
